Why Tidewater?

Our instructors are Red Cross certified with an average of eight years of teaching experience. We keep class sizes small so every swimmer gets individual attention, and our heated indoor facility means lessons run year-round — rain, shine, or hurricane season.

Over 2,000 Norfolk families have learned to swim with us since 2009. We would love to welcome yours.
