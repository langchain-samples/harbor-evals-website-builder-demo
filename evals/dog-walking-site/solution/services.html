Services

Three ways to book. All prices are per dog, and the second dog from the same household is half price.

Midday Walk

$28 per walk

Thirty minutes on a neighbourhood loop between eleven and two. The standard booking, and the one most dogs are on.

Trail Outing

$45 per outing

Ninety minutes on Sanitas or Chautauqua with a group of three, including transport both ways and a towel-off before we hand your dog back.

Puppy Visit

$22 per visit

Twenty minutes for dogs under six months: a short walk, a water change, and ten minutes of leash practice or plain company.

How billing works

We invoice at the end of each month for the walks that happened. Cancel by nine in the morning and there is no charge; after that we charge half, because the slot is gone.
