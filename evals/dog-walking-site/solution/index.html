Trailhead Dog Walking

Midday walks and trail outings for Boulder dogs, run by two people who would rather be outside anyway.

Why book us

Small groups, never more than four dogs, matched by pace rather than size. Your dog goes out with the same walker every time, so nobody has to relearn the leash manners each week.

You get a note after every walk with the route, how long you were out, and anything worth knowing — a limp, a new dog on the block, an empty water bowl we topped up.

Where we walk

Neighbourhood loops through Newlands and Mapleton Hill on weekday middays, and longer outings on the Sanitas and Chautauqua trails when the ground is dry. In heat above eighty-five we move to shade and shorten the route rather than cancel.
