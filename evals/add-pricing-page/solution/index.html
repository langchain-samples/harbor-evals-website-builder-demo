Books that close on time

Monthly bookkeeping for small construction firms and trades contractors in the Twin Cities. We reconcile, we categorise, and we hand your accountant a clean set of books on the fifth of every month.

What you get

Bank and card reconciliation, job costing that ties back to your estimates, payroll journal entries, and a monthly close package with a P&L you can actually read. If a transaction looks wrong, we ask before we file it.

Who we work with

Firms doing between four hundred thousand and six million a year, usually with one office manager and no in-house accountant. We are not the right fit if you need audited statements or fractional CFO work.
