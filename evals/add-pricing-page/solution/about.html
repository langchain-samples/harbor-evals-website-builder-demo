About Meridian

Two bookkeepers, eleven years of construction billing between them, and a strong preference for closing the month before the month is over.

How we started

Dana spent six years as an office manager at a mechanical contractor and got tired of watching job costs get reconstructed in March. Meridian opened in 2019 to do that work monthly instead of annually, for firms too small to hire it in-house.

Meridian Bookkeeping, Saint Paul, Minnesota.
