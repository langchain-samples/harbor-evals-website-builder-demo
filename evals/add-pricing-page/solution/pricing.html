Pricing

Flat monthly fees, quoted from your transaction volume and the number of accounts we reconcile. No hourly billing and no charge for the questions we ask you during the month.

Reconcile

$450 per month

Bank and card reconciliation for up to three accounts, monthly categorisation, and a P&L and balance sheet by the fifth. For firms under a million a year with straightforward billing.

Job Cost

$850 per month

Everything in Reconcile, plus job costing tied back to your estimates, committed-cost tracking on open jobs, and payroll journal entries. This is what most contractors we work with are on.

Close

$1,400 per month

Everything in Job Cost, plus WIP schedules, retainage tracking, and a monthly call to walk your accountant through the close package before it goes out.

What is not included

Tax preparation, audited statements, and fractional CFO work. We hand clean books to whoever does those, and we are happy to recommend three firms in Saint Paul who do.
